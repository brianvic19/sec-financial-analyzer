CAUSATION DISCLAIMER & METHODOLOGY NOTE

Statistical correlation or co-movement between historical stock prices and MD&A linguistic sentiment DOES NOT prove causation. Management narrative in SEC Form 10-K Item 7 reflects backward-looking explanations of past operational results alongside conservative, forward-looking risk management disclosures. Equity closing prices reflect discounted expected future cash flows, macroeconomic interest rate cycles, industry competition, and broad market sentiment. Co-movement indicates informational alignment, not direct causal dependency.

Academic Methodology & Disclosure Note

Primary vs. Peer Registrant Scope: Apple Inc. (AAPL) and Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) serve as the primary research subjects with full dual-axis linguistic analysis integrating Loughran-McDonald Item 7 MD&A sentiment scores against stock valuation movements. Microsoft Corporation (MSFT) is incorporated as the audited 10-year US-GAAP financial and ratio benchmark. Because Item 7 narrative extraction for Microsoft's legacy Form 10-K filings exhibited format variances across SEC EDGAR submission accessions, MD&A sentiment metrics for MSFT are designated as unavailable (—) in accordance with accounting conservatism standards rather than displaying unverified counts.

Reliability & Accounting Integrity Standards

  • Official SEC Data: Audited financial figures are retrieved directly from official SEC XBRL company facts filed under US-GAAP taxonomy.
  • Period-End Matching: Financial statement line items are matched strictly to the annual reporting period end date (June 30) with annual durations (300–420 days), preventing prior-year comparative row misattribution.
  • Balance Sheet Identity: Total Assets equals Total Liabilities plus Stockholders' Equity across all 10 fiscal periods (discrepancy = $0.0M).
  • Zero Server Latency: Precomputed and bundled for 100% offline static compatibility on GitHub Pages.
Methodology & SEC Data Architecture
Technical workflow, algorithms, dictionary categorization, and data normalization standards.

1. SEC EDGAR Programmatic Extraction

The backend engine (sec_downloader.py) interfaces directly with the SEC EDGAR Submissions API. All HTTP requests adhere to SEC access policies: User-Agent identification containing contact email and name, strictly throttled to fewer than 10 requests per second. Filings metadata is fetched to discover the 10 most recent primary Form 10-K filings for CIK 0000320193 (Apple) and CIK 0000002488 (AMD).

2. Item 7 (MD&A) Regex Extraction

HTML filings are parsed using regular expression state machines to capture the narrative text between Item 7 (Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations) and Item 7A (Quantitative and Qualitative Disclosures About Market Risk). Embedded HTML tags, XML tags, CSS styles, and XBRL metadata are stripped to leave clean plain text.

3. Loughran-McDonald Financial Sentiment Dictionary

Unlike general-purpose sentiment lexicons (which misclassify standard financial terms such as "tax", "cost", or "liability" as negative), the Loughran-McDonald Master Dictionary (March 2026 CSV) is calibrated specifically for accounting disclosures. The sentiment classification calculates:

Positive / Negative Sentiment Ratio = Total Matched Positive Words / Total Matched Negative Words
Net Sentiment per 1,000 Words = [(Total Positive Words - Total Negative Words) / Total MD&A Words] × 1,000

4. Stock Price Split Adjustments & Calendar Rollbacks

Stock closing prices are aligned with the exact fiscal year-end balance sheet dates. When a fiscal year-end falls on a non-trading weekend or holiday (e.g. Apple's last Saturday of September or AMD's Christmas Eve early closure), the algorithm automatically rolls back to the immediate preceding market trading day. Prices are split-adjusted to account for stock splits, most notably Apple's 4-for-1 split executed on August 31, 2020.
